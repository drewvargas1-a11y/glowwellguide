Silk sleep headbands solve a specific overnight problem cotton can't — cotton fibers create friction against hair and skin that leads to frizz, breakage, and skincare product transfer, while silk's smooth surface lets hair and skin glide instead of catching.

We compared silk grade (momme rating), closure style, and width, since a headband too narrow won't keep hair fully back, and one too tight will leave marks or headaches by morning.
