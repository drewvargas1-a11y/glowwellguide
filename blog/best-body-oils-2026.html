Body oils work differently than lotions — they sit closer to skin's own sebum composition and can lock in moisture more effectively when applied to slightly damp skin right after a shower. We compared absorption speed (nobody wants to wait 20 minutes before getting dressed), scent, and specific benefits like scar or stretch mark treatment.

Pricing spans from an affordable daily spray to a genuine luxury bottle, so there's an entry point regardless of how much of a routine you want to build around it.
