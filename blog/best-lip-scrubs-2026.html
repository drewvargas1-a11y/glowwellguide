Lip scrubs work through gentle physical exfoliation (usually fine sugar granules) combined with moisturizing oils and butters, so you're removing flaky buildup and hydrating in the same step rather than scrubbing skin dry. We compared grain fineness, moisture level, and flavor.

Use these 1-2 times a week rather than daily — over-exfoliating already-sensitive lip skin can cause more irritation than it solves.
