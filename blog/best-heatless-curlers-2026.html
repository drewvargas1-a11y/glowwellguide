Curling irons and wands can be hard on hair over time, especially if you style daily. Heatless curlers swap heat for time: hair sets in shape while you sleep, so you skip the morning styling session entirely.

Results depend on your hair length and texture, and on how damp your hair is when you wrap it. The best kits are soft enough to sleep on comfortably and come with scrunchies or clips to hold everything in place.
