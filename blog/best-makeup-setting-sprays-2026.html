Setting sprays work by creating a light film over finished makeup that locks everything in place — preventing creasing into fine lines, fading throughout the day, and makeup migrating from oil breakthrough. We compared claimed wear time, finish (matte vs. dewy vs. natural), and oil control specifically for combination and oily skin.

Application technique matters as much as the product — a fine, even mist held at the right distance makes a bigger difference than which specific spray you're using, within reason.
