Frequently Asked Questions

Dot color directly onto the apples of your cheeks, then blend outward and upward with clean fingertips (body heat helps it melt into skin) — a brush works too, but fingers typically give the most seamless, natural result.
Many formulas are specifically designed for multi-use — check the product description, since some are cheek-only and may not perform as well or feel as comfortable on lips.
Quality formulas typically last 4-8 hours before needing a touch-up, though oily skin types may see faster fading than dry or normal skin — setting with a light powder can extend wear.
