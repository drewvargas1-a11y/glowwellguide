Niacinamide serums vary mainly by concentration (5-12%) and what's paired alongside it — zinc for oil control, hyaluronic acid for hydration, or tranexamic acid for extra brightening. Higher concentration isn't automatically better; it depends on your skin's tolerance and specific goal.

We compared formulas across sensitivity levels, since niacinamide is generally gentle but percentage and added actives change how well different skin types tolerate daily use.

#1 · Best Overall

The Ordinary Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1%

~$12
🏆 The category standard · zinc for oil control · budget price

The Ordinary's niacinamide serum essentially defined this category's mainstream popularity, and it remains the benchmark for a reason — a straightforward 10% niacinamide + 1% zinc formula that visibly reduces the look of blemishes and balances sebum without unnecessary extras.

It's especially well suited to blemish-prone and oily skin thanks to the zinc, and the water-based, lightweight texture layers easily under moisturizer or makeup without pilling.
