The right hand cream depends on how severe your dryness is. A light, fast-absorbing cream works fine for daily maintenance between hand washes; genuinely cracked, work-worn hands need a denser, more occlusive formula that actually seals in moisture through repeated washing.

We compared absorption speed, greasiness, scent, and how well each formula held up through a day of frequent hand washing — the real test for any winter hand cream.

Frequently Asked Questions

Hands have fewer oil glands than most other body areas and are exposed to more friction, washing, and cold air directly, so they lose moisture faster and show winter dryness earlier and more severely.
After every hand washing is ideal for genuinely dry or cracked hands — moisture loss is highest right after washing, so that's the most effective time to reapply.
Look for shea butter, glycerin, ceramides, or dimethicone for occlusive sealing, plus soothing ingredients like allantoin if skin is also irritated or painful from cracking.
