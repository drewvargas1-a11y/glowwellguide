Tinted moisturizers even out skin tone without the mask-like feel of foundation. They won't cover everything, but they blur redness and give skin a healthy, natural finish.

Many now include broad-spectrum SPF. If you rely on it as your only sunscreen, apply a generous amount and reapply during long days outdoors.
