The right hair oil does three things simultaneously: seals the hair cuticle (which reduces frizz and prevents moisture loss), adds optical shine, and delivers nourishing fatty acids that strengthen the strand from within. The wrong one weighs hair down, makes it look greasy, or doesn't absorb at all.

We tested these four oils across straight, wavy, curly, damaged, and color-treated hair types — evaluating weight, absorption speed, shine quality, frizz control, and how well each performed as both a pre-wash treatment and a finishing product.

Moroccanoil Treatment is the product that single-handedly made argan oil mainstream. #2 · Best for Damaged / Color-Treated

Olaplex No.7 Bonding Oil

~$28
💡 Bis-aminopropyl diglycol dimaleate bond repair

Olaplex No.7 Bonding Oil is unique among hair oils because it doesn't just coat the hair shaft — it works inside the strand to repair broken disulfide bonds that are damaged by bleach, chemical treatments, heat styling, and environmental stress. The key ingredient, bis-aminopropyl diglycol dimaleate (Olaplex's patented active), physically reconnects broken bonds within the hair's cortex — the only product category that does this outside of a salon chair.

The ultra-lightweight formula (2–6 drops is a full application) provides up to 350°F heat protection, adds shine, and dramatically reduces styling time on damaged hair by smoothing the cuticle. The molecular weight of the oil is extremely small, so it penetrates rather than sits on top. Best for: bleached hair, keratin-treated hair, chemically relaxed hair, or any hair that's lost its elasticity and shine. One of the best-selling hair products on Amazon at 4.6 stars.

Frequently Asked Questions

Both work, for different purposes. Pre-wash treatment (apply to dry hair 30–60 minutes before shampooing) protects hair from hygral fatigue and reduces protein loss during washing — ideal for damaged or porous hair. Post-wash application (on damp or dry hair after styling) adds shine, seals the cuticle, and tames frizz. For daily use, post-wash is most practical and provides the results most people are looking for.
Hair oils are formulated from natural oils (argan, squalane, marula) and coat the hair shaft to seal moisture and add shine. Hair serums are typically silicone-based or water-based with active ingredients targeting specific concerns like damage repair or heat protection. Oils are generally heavier and more nourishing; serums are lighter with more precision styling hold. Many people use both — serum first for structure, oil last for finish.
Hair oil applied to the lengths doesn't cause hair loss. However, heavy oils applied directly to the scalp and left without thorough washing can clog follicles over time, potentially contributing to dandruff or folliculitis. For scalp application, use lightweight oils (squalane, jojoba) and wash within 24 hours. A monthly clarifying shampoo prevents buildup from any styling product, including oils.
