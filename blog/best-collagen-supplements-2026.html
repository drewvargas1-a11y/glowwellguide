Collagen is the most abundant protein in the human body — it makes up 75–80% of skin's dry weight and forms the structural matrix of joints, tendons, and hair follicles. After age 25, natural production declines by about 1% per year, and by 40 you're producing roughly 15% less than peak. Supplementing with hydrolyzed collagen peptides has strong clinical backing for improving skin elasticity, reducing fine lines, and supporting joint comfort.

We evaluated four collagen products across source quality, hydrolyzation method, collagen types present, additional ingredients, and actual taste/mixability — because the best supplement is the one you'll actually take daily.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most people notice initial improvements in skin hydration within 4–6 weeks. Visible skin elasticity improvements and nail strength typically appear at 8–12 weeks. Joint and hair results take the longest — 3–6 months of consistent daily intake. For best results, pair collagen with vitamin C (required for collagen synthesis) and take daily without skipping.
Clinical studies showing skin and joint benefits typically use 2.5g–10g of hydrolyzed collagen peptides per day. Most supplements provide 10–20g per serving — this exceeds clinical dosage, which is fine, but you don't need more than 10g for measurable results. More than 20g per day provides no additional benefit and just adds unnecessary cost.
