Hydrocolloid is the same material used in wound dressings. It absorbs fluid from surfaced whiteheads and protects the spot from picking and bacteria, which can help it heal faster and look flatter by morning.

Patches work best on pimples with a visible head. For deep, painful cystic acne or persistent breakouts, see a dermatologist.
