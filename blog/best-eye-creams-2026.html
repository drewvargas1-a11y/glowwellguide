Eye creams address concerns regular moisturizer usually can't reach as effectively — the thin under-eye skin needs targeted ingredients like caffeine (de-puffing), retinol (fine lines), and niacinamide (brightening) in a gentler concentration than you'd use on the rest of your face.

We compared formulas by primary concern addressed, since "best eye cream" really depends on whether your main issue is dark circles, puffiness, or early fine lines.
