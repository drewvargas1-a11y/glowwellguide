TRESemmé Dry Shampoo Spray, 6-Pack

🏆 6-pack · natural tapioca & citrus extracts · budget-friendly

TRESemmé's formula uses natural tapioca and citrus extracts to remove excess oil, impurities, and odor without the heavy fragrance some drugstore dry shampoos rely on to mask a lack of real cleaning power. At 6 cans for the price, it's the easiest one to keep in constant rotation.

It brushes out cleanly with minimal white cast on most hair colors, and the Warm Petals scent is light rather than overpowering. For anyone using dry shampoo several times a week, the 6-pack format alone makes this the practical default.
