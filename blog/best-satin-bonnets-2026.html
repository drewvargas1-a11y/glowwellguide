Bonnets are a staple for curly, coily, and textured hair, and they help any hair type that frizzes or tangles overnight. Satin's smooth surface reduces friction and helps hair hold moisture.

The biggest differences are fit (adjustable bands stay on better), size (large bonnets fit braids and long hair), and layers (double-layer bonnets are more durable).
