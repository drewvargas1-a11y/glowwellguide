Facial oils work best as a finishing step — a few drops pressed into skin after your regular moisturizer to lock in hydration and add a natural radiance, not necessarily a moisturizer replacement on their own. We compared absorption speed, greasy residue, and specific benefits like brightening or anti-aging.

The right pick depends partly on skin type — lighter oils suit combination or acne-prone skin, while richer botanical blends work best for genuinely dry or mature skin that wants more intensive nourishment.
