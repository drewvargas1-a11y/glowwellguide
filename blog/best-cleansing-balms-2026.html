A cleansing balm is a solid oil cleanser. Massaged onto dry skin, it turns into an oil that dissolves makeup and SPF; add water and it emulsifies into a milk that rinses away.

Follow with a gentle water-based cleanser for a full double cleanse, especially if you wear long-wear makeup or sunscreen daily.
