Vitamin E oil is sold in a range of potencies (measured in IU) and purity levels, from 100% pure d-alpha tocopherol to lighter facial-oil formulations blended for easier daily use. We compared IU concentration, texture, and whether each is better suited for spot treatment or all-over use.

Higher IU isn't automatically better for regular facial use — very high concentrations can feel heavy or greasy on the face, while a lighter formulated oil works better for daily wear.
