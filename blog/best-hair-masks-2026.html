Hair masks work by sitting on strands longer than rinse-out conditioner, giving actives like keratin, argan oil, or collagen real time to penetrate the hair shaft rather than just coating the surface. We compared repair claims, scent, and how each formula performed on dry, damaged, or color-treated hair specifically.

Most of these are designed for weekly use — layering a mask into an already-good routine, not replacing daily conditioner entirely.
