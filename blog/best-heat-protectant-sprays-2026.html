Use a heat protectant every time you blow-dry, straighten, or curl. Spray evenly over damp or dry hair (depending on the product), comb through, and let it absorb before using hot tools.

Brands often list a maximum temperature, commonly up to 450°F. Even so, using a lower heat setting is the best way to protect your hair.
