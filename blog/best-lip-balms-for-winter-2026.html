The right winter lip balm depends on how bad the damage already is. A light beeswax balm is fine for daily prevention; genuinely cracked or bleeding lips need a denser repair ointment with occlusive ingredients that create a real barrier against wind and cold.

We compared ingredient lists, SPF options, and how long each formula actually lasted through a dry, windy day before needing reapplication.
