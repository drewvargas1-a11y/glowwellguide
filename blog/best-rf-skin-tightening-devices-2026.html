Frequently Asked Questions

How does RF skin tightening actually work?

RF devices deliver electromagnetic energy into the dermis, generating heat in the 40–45°C range. This immediately contracts existing collagen fibers (subtle instant firming) and triggers fibroblast activity to produce new collagen over 8–12 weeks. At-home devices use lower energy than professional treatments — consistent use over months is required to see comparable cumulative results. Unlike IPL or laser, RF uses heat rather than light so it is safe for all Fitzpatrick skin tones.

Is at-home RF skin tightening safe for all skin tones?

Yes — RF is generally safe for all skin tones including deeper tones (Fitzpatrick V–VI) that cannot use IPL. Contraindications include active skin infections, metal implants, pacemakers, pregnancy, and active cancer in the treatment area. Always start on the lowest setting, apply coupling gel as directed, and keep the device continuously moving to prevent localized heat buildup.

How long before you see RF results?

Texture improvements appear within 2–4 weeks with consistent use (2–3×/week). Visible lifting from new collagen formation typically becomes noticeable at 8–12 weeks, with peak results at 3–6 months. Consistency matters more than session frequency — 2 sessions/week for 12 weeks outperforms 5 sessions/week for 4 weeks for overall collagen remodeling outcomes.
