Personal facial humidifiers use nano-atomization to turn water into an ultra-fine mist your skin actually absorbs, rather than beading up and evaporating like a coarser spray. We compared tank size, mist fineness, and battery life since those determine how practical each one is for daily desk or travel use.

None of these replace a full room humidifier for genuinely dry indoor air, but for targeted refreshes through a dry winter workday, they're a genuinely useful category.
