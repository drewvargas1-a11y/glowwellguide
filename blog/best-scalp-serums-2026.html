Frequently Asked Questions

Scalp serums deliver concentrated actives directly to follicles, bypassing the dilution from washing. Different formulas address different concerns: caffeine serums stimulate blood flow, exfoliating serums (AHA/BHA) dissolve buildup, and peptide/protein serums strengthen follicle anchoring. Applied after washing to a clean scalp, they absorb more effectively than products applied to dirty hair.
Most leave-in scalp serums (caffeine, peptide) are designed for daily use. Exfoliating scalp treatments containing AHAs or salicylic acid should be used 2–3 times per week to avoid over-stripping natural oils. Always apply to a clean scalp for maximum absorption — the pores are open after washing and actives penetrate more effectively.
Quality scalp serums should not cause hair loss — they're designed to improve follicle health. However, high-alcohol formulas can be drying. If you notice increased shedding after starting a serum, stop and consult a dermatologist. Initial shedding in the first 2–4 weeks of caffeine serums is sometimes reported as dormant follicles activate — this is typically temporary.
