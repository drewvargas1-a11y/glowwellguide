Best Scalp Scrubs (2026)

Your scalp collects dry shampoo, styling products, oil, and dead skin, and regular shampoo doesn't always lift it all. A scalp scrub once or twice a week resets things. We compared exfoliants, ingredients, and how each fits a wash routine.

Scalp scrubs use physical exfoliants (sea salt, sugar, or micro-beads from plants) and sometimes chemical exfoliants to clear away buildup at the roots. Many people notice hair feels lighter and looks fresher for longer afterward.

Scrubs aren't for daily use: once or twice a week is plenty for most scalps. If you have a scalp condition, persistent dandruff, or irritation, check with a dermatologist first.
