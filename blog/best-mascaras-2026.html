Mascara comes down to three things: the brush, which shapes how formula lands on each lash; the formula, which decides length, volume, and hold; and wear, meaning whether it smudges or flakes.

Washable formulas are easier to remove and gentler for everyday wear. Waterproof formulas hold curl better and survive rain and tears, but need an oil-based remover.
