Good cuticle oil does two things: softens hardened, cracked skin around the nail, and delivers nutrients (vitamin E, jojoba, almond oil) that support nail growth and strength over time. We compared absorption speed, portability, and how well each formula held up through repeated daily use.

Format matters as much as ingredients here — a dropper bottle is great for a nightly routine at home, while a pen format is what actually gets used throughout a busy day.
