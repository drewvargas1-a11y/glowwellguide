Under-eye patches work differently from eye creams: instead of sitting on top of the skin, hydrogel patches create a sealed delivery system that drives active ingredients deep into the thin under-eye skin. The occlusive patch prevents evaporation, creates slight pressure that boosts absorption, and the hydrogel itself delivers a concentrated bolus of actives in a single treatment. The result: visible improvement in puffiness and hydration in as little as 5 minutes.

We tested four options — covering the range from $10 budget packs to $52 luxury 30-pair sets — and ranked them on immediate depuffing visible on camera, 24-hour hydration retention, and ingredient quality.
