Hyaluronic acid (HA) is the most popular ingredient in skincare — and one of the most misunderstood. HA doesn't add moisture to your skin; it acts as a humectant, drawing and holding water from your environment and the deeper layers of your skin. The molecule is naturally produced by your body but declines significantly with age.

The key to effective HA: molecular weight. Different sizes penetrate to different depths. The best serums use multiple weights simultaneously. We tested four formulas on dehydrated, normal, and oily skin types over 4 weeks. Here's how they stack up.

A serum with multiple molecular weights is most effective. High-molecular-weight HA sits on the skin surface for immediate plumping. Low-molecular-weight HA penetrates deeper into the dermis for structural hydration. The best serums contain both — look for products listing multiple forms of sodium hyaluronate, or that specifically note "multi-weight" or "3 types of HA."
