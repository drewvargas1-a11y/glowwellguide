Most skincare doesn't require refrigeration, but cooling feels great on puffy morning skin and can help some products, like vitamin C serums, stay fresh longer once opened.

Beauty fridges use quiet thermoelectric cooling and typically hold products around 50°F or so below room temperature. Many can also switch to a warming mode.
