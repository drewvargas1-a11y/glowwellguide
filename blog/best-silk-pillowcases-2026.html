Cotton pillowcases create friction that roughs up the hair cuticle and tugs at skin as you move in your sleep. Silk's smooth surface lets hair glide, which can mean less frizz and fewer tangles in the morning, and it doesn't absorb skincare products the way cotton does.

The key numbers are momme (fabric weight; higher is denser and more durable) and silk grade (6A is the highest). We focused on 100% mulberry silk on both sides.

Frequently Asked Questions

Silk is a natural protein fiber; satin is a weave, usually polyester. Both reduce friction, but silk is more breathable and doesn't trap heat the way polyester satin can.
19–22 momme is the sweet spot. Higher momme is denser and more durable; lighter silk feels cooler but wears faster.
Hand wash or machine wash on a delicate cycle in a mesh bag with cool water and a silk-safe detergent, then air dry out of direct sun.
Silk causes less tugging on skin than cotton, which may reduce sleep creases, but it isn't a treatment for wrinkles.
