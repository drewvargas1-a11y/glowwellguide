Frequently Asked Questions

Winter moisturizers typically contain more occlusive ingredients (shea butter, ceramides, petrolatum) to seal in moisture against dry, cold air and indoor heating, versus lighter lotions or gels used in humid summer months. They also tend to skip mattifying ingredients that can worsen dryness.
Many people benefit from a lighter formula in summer and a richer, more occlusive one in winter, especially if you notice increased tightness, flaking, or sensitivity once heating season starts. Sensitive or very dry skin may need the richer formula year-round.
Choose a non-comedogenic formula to minimize this risk — all four picks here are labeled non-comedogenic. If you're prone to breakouts, start with the lighter niacinamide option rather than the richest shea butter formula.
