Frequently Asked Questions

Do microcurrent eye devices actually reduce fine lines?

Microcurrent devices like the NuFACE FIX have meaningful clinical evidence for short-term fine line reduction and facial muscle toning. Low-level electrical current (35–500 microamps) stimulates facial muscles, improving tone and smoothing overlying skin. Studies show cumulative improvement over 4–12 weeks of consistent use (typically 5 days/week). Results are real but modest — microcurrent smooths and tones, but doesn't replicate the effects of injectables or professional treatments. It's best understood as a non-invasive maintenance tool.

What causes dark circles and under-eye puffiness?

Puffiness is caused by fluid retention — lymphatic and blood fluid pooling overnight, worsened by salt, alcohol, allergies, and poor sleep. Eye massage devices address puffiness through lymphatic drainage stimulation. Dark circles have multiple causes: thin skin revealing blood vessels (genetic), hyperpigmentation (sun damage), or volume-loss shadows. Devices can improve vascular/fluid dark circles; structural or pigmentation dark circles need targeted skincare or professional treatment.

How often should you use an eye massager or microcurrent device?

Microcurrent (NuFACE FIX): 5 days/week initially, then 2–3 times/week for maintenance. Massage-based devices (Foreo IRIS 2, TOUCHBeauty): daily use is safe. Morning use is most effective for puffiness (addresses overnight accumulation). Avoid over active breakouts, irritated skin, or immediately after injectables.
