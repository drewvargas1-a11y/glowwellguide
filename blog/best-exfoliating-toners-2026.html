AHAs like glycolic acid work on the surface for smoother, brighter-looking skin. BHAs like salicylic acid are oil-soluble and get into pores, which makes them popular for blackheads and breakouts.

Start slowly, a few times a week, and always wear sunscreen; acids can increase sun sensitivity.
